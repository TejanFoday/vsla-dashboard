Study Overview

All Respondents
About this study: The Embassy of Ireland commissioned this comprehensive VSLA assessment across Sierra Leone in December 2025, examining how VSLAs drive transformative change across gender equality, governance, and food security. 610 respondents were surveyed across 8 districts and 40 communities using a mixed-methods, gender-responsive approach.

Governance & Civic Participation

All Respondents
Key finding: VSLA members demonstrate significantly higher civic engagement, with 93% invited to community planning activities and 92.3% of women reporting they feel safer expressing community concerns. A striking governance transformation: only 11% rated their governance participation as "High" before VSLA — this rose to 55% after.

Food Security & Nutrition

All Respondents
Key finding: While food insecurity remains prevalent (65% experienced shortage in the last 12 months), VSLA membership is a powerful mitigating factor. 95% report improved ability to buy nutritious foods, 93.2% increased agricultural production, and 90.5% received nutrition training through their VSLA.

Respondent Profile

All Respondents
610 respondents across 4 regions and 8 districts, with 84.1% female reflecting the gender composition of VSLA membership. The majority are farmers or petty traders aged 25–64, with over 44% having no formal education — highlighting VSLA's role in reaching the most marginalized.
